> ... I do remember that in the first Sangha in France, in Sceaux south of Paris, Thich Nhat Hanh instore as a routine that, after diner the Sangha should practice singing together or individualy. In 1972 our best singer was Laura, Hasler who knows all the songs of Joan Baez. Then was Moby Ho, american young women who was the translator of the books of Thay. Then Neige, a French and - anyway - all of the first members of the international Sangha, Sudarat was singing in Thaï and we enjoy a lot. One day, when I was speaking with Thay alone in his bedroom, I ask him : "Thay why you introduce this daily practice every evening. With so wonderfull smile, Thay told me : Pierre, did you notice that, every morning our Sister Chan Khong open all the letters from Vietnam. 80% of those letters are so sad about the war that was ragging, that our sister suffer so much by compassion, that she cry and cry and cry. This broke my heart every time. Did you notice Pierre, that our sister is not able to cry and to sing Together ? So I decide that, in order to restore peace in the heart of Sister Chan Khong to have a good sleep, I have taken the decision, that songs from each one will cure our suffering and, it seams, that it's work isn't ? Our Thay is so wise, he is so move by love that he always find to teach us a way to restore peace and joy in our heart....  I wrote this tonight because I didn't read it in any books yet ! It's start in 1972 and is still practice in all Plum Village worldwide. Love and gratitude goes to Thay. Being a guitarist, my first contribution to raise funds for the orphans in Vietnam was to organise folk's songs concert with my friends Graeme Allwright, George Moustaki, Marcel Dadi, or Maxime Le Forestier and so many as Joan Baez and - of course - our "star" : Sister Chan Khong !
